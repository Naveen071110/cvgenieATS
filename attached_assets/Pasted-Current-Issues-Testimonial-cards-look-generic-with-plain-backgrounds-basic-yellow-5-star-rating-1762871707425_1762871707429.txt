Needs to look more premium, credible, and visually interesting in BOTH light and dark modes.

Testimonial Card Background & Depth

Replace the plain background on each testimonial card with a subtle blue-to-purple brand gradient (e.g., #5A5AFF to #A259FF or similar).

Add a subtle shadow AND a 1px semi-transparent border for depth.

Make sure gradients and shadows look good in both dark and light modes (test both; adjust saturation/opacity as needed).

Star Ratings

Enhance stars with a soft glow effect and/or animation on hover (e.g., increase brightness, add a gentle scale or pulse).

Make stars visually distinctive in both themes.

Testimonial Text

Change quote/testimonial text to bright white (#FFFFFF) for both light and dark modes, or use a dark primary color for light mode and white for dark mode if theme toggling.

Avatars

Actual user photos (if available) OR

More sophisticated, brand-matching illustrated avatars (use an avatar library or SVGs).

Add a verified badge/checkmark icon (styled in brand colors) next to/overlaid on each avatar for trust.

Company Logos/Icons

Show a small company logo or industry icon below/beside the user’s designation/job title.

Keep logos in grayscale or softly colored (adapt for both modes).

Use a placeholder icon if no logo is available.

Section Title and Subtitle

Make the section title (“Real People. Real Interview Success.”) larger, bolder, and use a font/weight that matches your branding.

Subtitle should stand out from both the card backgrounds and the page background, in both color modes.

Section Background
