# ATS Resume Guide: How to Beat Applicant Tracking Systems in 2025

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are used by over 90% of Fortune 500 companies to filter resumes before they reach human recruiters. Understanding how these systems work is crucial for job seekers in 2025.

## What is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System is software that helps employers manage job applications by automatically scanning, parsing, and ranking resumes based on specific criteria.

## Key ATS Optimization Strategies

### 1. Use Standard Formatting

- **Stick to standard fonts**: Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman
- **Avoid complex layouts**: No tables, text boxes, or graphics
- **Use standard section headers**: "Work Experience", "Education", "Skills"

### 2. Keyword Optimization

Match your resume to the job description by including relevant keywords naturally throughout your content.

### 3. File Format Matters

Always submit your resume in **DOCX** or **TXT** format for maximum ATS compatibility. PDF files can sometimes cause parsing issues.

## Common ATS Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Using headers and footers** - ATS software often ignores these sections
2. **Including graphics or images** - These elements confuse parsing algorithms
3. **Unusual section names** - Stick to conventional headings
